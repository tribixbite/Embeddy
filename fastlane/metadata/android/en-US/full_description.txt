Embeddy is a media toolkit for getting images and video into the size and format
that chat apps, forums and social sites actually accept.

All conversion and compression runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless
you explicitly choose the Upload tool.

<b>Convert</b>
Turn video, GIF or animated WebP into animated WebP. An adaptive quality pass
starts at your chosen quality and steps down until the file fits a size target,
so you get the best quality that still uploads.

• Presets for Discord (10 MB), Telegram stickers (256 KB) and Slack (5 MB)
• Trim to a range, or stitch several segments together
• Lanczos downscaling, optional sharpening, denoise and dithering
• Exact output dimensions with centre-crop, or scale to a maximum size
• Generate a 3-second preview before committing to a full encode

<b>Squoosh</b>
Compress still images to WebP, JPEG, PNG or AVIF with a before/after slider so
you can see exactly what a quality setting costs.

<b>Inspect</b>
See how a link will look when shared: Open Graph and Twitter Card tags rendered
as a live preview card. Also reads local files — EXIF, camera settings, GPS,
codec, colour space, bit depth and keyframe cadence.

<b>Upload</b>
Send a file to an anonymous host (0x0.st or catbox.moe) and get a link back.
EXIF is stripped before upload when the format supports it.

<b>Privacy</b>
No analytics, no accounts, no ads. The only network access is the Inspect tool
fetching a URL you typed, and the Upload tool sending a file you chose.
