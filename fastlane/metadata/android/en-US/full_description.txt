Embeddy is a quick video editor and media toolkit: trim, join and convert clips,
and get images and video into the size and format that chat apps, forums and
social sites actually accept.

All editing, conversion and compression runs on your device. Nothing is
uploaded unless you explicitly choose the Upload tool.

<b>Edit</b>
• Trim to a range, or cut several segments and stitch them together
• Join separate videos into one, in any order, each with its own trim
• Speed from 0.25× to 4×, rotate, flip, crop and reverse
• Per-segment volume, mute, or extract the audio track as M4A
• Grab a still frame and compress it in Squoosh
• Turn a set of photos into an animation or a video

<b>Convert</b>
Export MP4, the original container, animated WebP or GIF. For WebP and GIF an
adaptive quality pass steps down until the file fits a size target; for MP4 the
bitrate is computed from the target, so you get the best quality that still
uploads.

• Presets for Discord (10 MB), Telegram stickers (256 KB) and Slack (5 MB)
• Keep the original resolution and frame rate, or scale down
• Lanczos downscaling, optional sharpening, denoise and dithering
• Generate a 3-second preview before committing to a full encode
• Location and other metadata are removed from exported video

<b>Squoosh</b>
Compress still images to WebP, JPEG, PNG or AVIF with a before/after slider so
you can see exactly what a quality setting costs.

<b>Inspect</b>
See how a link will look when shared: Open Graph and Twitter Card tags rendered
as a live preview card. Also reads local files — EXIF, camera settings, GPS,
codec, colour space, bit depth and keyframe cadence.

<b>Upload</b>
Send a file to an anonymous host (0x0.st or catbox.moe) and get a link back.
EXIF is stripped before upload when the format supports it.

<b>Privacy</b>
No analytics, no accounts, no ads, and no access to your photo library — you
pick each file yourself. The only network access is the Inspect tool fetching a
URL you typed, and the Upload tool sending a file you chose.
Privacy policy: https://embeddy.link/privacy/
